Control contrast and visual tone

The dark color paints active modules; the light color paints the remaining matrix and quiet zone. QR scanners generally perform best when dark modules are substantially darker than the light background. Transparency reveals whatever is behind the image, so real-world contrast may differ from the editor preview.

Solid fills use one dark color. Linear and radial gradients blend toward an ending color, while image fill uses a selected image beneath separate dark and light contrast overlays.

Gradients and image fills

Gradient angle controls the direction of a linear blend; radial gradients move outward from the center. Avoid combinations where part of the dark fill approaches the light color.

The recommended image setting overlays black at 25% on dark modules and white at 75% on light areas. It is a useful starting point, not a guarantee: detailed images and low-contrast regions still require scanning tests on the final device or paper.
