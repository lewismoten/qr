Add a recognizable center mark

Center artwork can use an uploaded logo, an emoji, editable pixel art, or the frame message. Artwork is composited into the final image and may cover data modules, so stronger error correction and a modest artwork size improve the chance of recovery.

Logos can use a protective background. Emoji and pixel art use a light outline around only their opaque artwork, allowing QR modules to remain visible through transparent areas. Pixel art can also follow the selected module shape so its cells share the QR code’s visual language.

Logo, emoji, and pixel-art controls

The size slider controls the percentage of the matrix occupied by the artwork. Uploaded logos remain in browser memory until cleared or the page closes. Emoji rendering depends on the selected font available on the device that creates the image.

The pixel editor supports an 8 to 32 cell grid, an EGA-inspired palette, custom colors, and transparent erasing. Fine artwork usually needs a larger QR output so both artwork pixels and QR modules remain distinct.
