Shape the QR matrix

Modules are the small dark and light cells that carry QR data. Square modules preserve the standard geometry; rounded, dotted, diamond, and custom modules trade some scanning tolerance for a more distinctive appearance.

Corner eyes are the three large finder patterns scanners use to locate and orient the code. Their outer rings and center blocks can be styled separately, but their overall contrast and recognizable structure should remain strong.

What the custom controls change

Rounding softens corners, inset creates space between neighboring cells, and rotation turns each module. Larger insets and extreme rotations remove more dark area and can make dense or physically small codes harder to scan.

Custom eye colors apply only when enabled. Test the final export at its intended printed or displayed size, especially when using center artwork, transparency, or a background image as well.
