Encode a precise place

Geographic QR codes use latitude and longitude to open a location in compatible mapping software. An optional label gives the coordinates a recognizable name.

Latitude must be between -90 and 90; longitude must be between -180 and 180. Five decimal places locate a point within roughly a meter under typical conditions.
