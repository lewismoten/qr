Geographic QR codes use latitude and longitude to open a location in compatible mapping software. An optional label gives the coordinates a recognizable name.

Latitude must be between -90 and 90; longitude must be between -180 and 180. Five decimal places locate a point within roughly a meter under typical conditions.

Use my location asks your browser for permission only when you select the button. If permission is granted, the returned latitude and longitude replace the fields and marker; a detailed active map also centers on that position. The application does not continuously track your location.

Browser-provided coordinates may be approximate, stale or incorrect, especially when GPS is unavailable or the estimate relies on network information. Check important coordinates before generating or sharing a QR code.
